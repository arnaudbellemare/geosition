Privacy notice for the GEOSITION pilot

For the GEOSITION hosted pilot and Vercel connection.

Who operates the pilot

The GEOSITION pilot is operated by Arnaud Bellemare. For privacy or support questions, contact info@gestionvelora.com.

Information we handle

When you use the pilot, we may handle your account email, a password hash, workspace membership, website domains and verification information, audit findings, page URLs, and information you or an authorized operator provide. An audit may collect information from the website you ask us to review. Do not put passwords, API keys, payment details or customer personal information in audit evidence or progress notes.

Vercel connection

When you connect Vercel, GEOSITION receives an authorization token and project and team identifiers. It uses them to check whether your verified website domain belongs to the selected Vercel project. The token is stored encrypted. Vercel displays the permissions requested during authorization. A connection alone does not verify an individual ChatGPT request or establish ongoing traffic monitoring.

How we use and share information

We use this information to provide accounts and workspaces, verify websites, perform requested audits, show results and setup progress, administer authorized connections, provide support and protect the service. Vercel provides hosting and the Vercel connection; Supabase provides the database. These providers process information as needed to supply their services. The pilot database is hosted in the United States; hosting and provider processing may occur outside your region. Other providers may be involved when you choose to use their services.

Cookies

GEOSITION uses session and security cookies to keep you signed in and protect requests. Your browser can control cookies, but disabling these cookies may prevent sign-in and other functionality.

Retention and requests

Audit data is kept until the business asks for deletion, including after account closure. For access, correction or deletion requests, email info@gestionvelora.com. We aim to respond within 30 days; the response will explain the next steps and any applicable limitations. A response within 30 days is not a promise that deletion, including any necessary handling of backups or legally retained information, will be completed within that time.

Security

GEOSITION uses access controls and encryption for saved Vercel connection tokens. No system can guarantee absolute security.
