## Design Context

### Users
Small-business owners and their operators use GEOSITION to prepare a business for AI discovery and commerce. They want the assistant to handle technical work and show them decisions that need their attention.

### Brand Personality
Clear and reassuring. Use plain, direct language and report what is actually verified.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow the existing GEOSITION platform's visual language for account and workspace screens. Keep connection and authorization screens calm, focused, and easy to scan.

### Design Principles
- Show the business and requesting service before asking for approval.
- Explain access and its limits in language a business owner understands.
- Keep credentials and technical identifiers out of ordinary progress displays.
- Make errors and next steps specific, accessible, and recoverable.
- Preserve distinction between reported progress and verified provider access.
