# GSC operator handbook

Operator-facing guidance for Google Search Console — segments, annotations, exports. **No fake metrics**: only paste figures copied from GSC or another approved tool.

## Segments

- Save reusable filters (e.g. landing page folder, country, device) as named comparisons only after validating they answer a recurring question.
- Prefer fewer, stable segments over one-off filters nobody revisits.

## Annotations

- Log deploys, template changes, `robots`/redirect moves, and major content merges on a timeline you control (GSC notes + external doc if needed).
- Tie annotations to decision IDs in `seo/decision-cards.md` when relevant.

## Exports

- Export tables when sharing externally; scrub internal-only URLs if necessary.
- Never round or “beautify” numbers when handing results to stakeholders — use exact exports.

## What not to do

- Do not screenshot fabricated KPIs.
- Do not mix Search Console queries with rank trackers without labeling sources.
